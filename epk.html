Short bio

Zane Deep, also known as AlphaCloudGod, is a Las Vegas, Nevada artist and producer blending melodic trap, G-funk, electronic and cinematic sound. He does not just release songs. He builds worlds for them: an interactive island, a deep-space dogfight, a Vegas boxing ring and a snowboarding mountain, all driven by his own music.

Full bio

Zane Deep, also known as AlphaCloudGod, is a multi-genre artist and producer based in Las Vegas, Nevada. His sound moves between melodic trap, G-funk, electronic and cinematic production, with a catalog that is growing across Spotify, YouTube and BandLab. His current release, Miami Twist Out, is out now on YouTube and TikTok. He has reached the Top 10 on ReverbNation as a Las Vegas trap rapper, had a track handpicked by BandLab's A&R team, who worked with him for over a week to release it, been backed by curators on Groover and SubmitHub, and worked with A&Rs along the way.

What sets him apart is how he presents the music. At zanedeep.vercel.app, every song lives inside something you can play: an island you can drive, a fighter jet in deep space, a boxing ring in a Vegas arena and a snowboarding run through three mountains, all running on a phone browser with no app to install. He also builds immersive worlds like these for other musicians. Zane Deep is open to a label partnership to take the music, and the world around it, to a much wider audience.
